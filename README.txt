# 🎓 Maharashtra Engineering College Predictor

A web-based tool that helps students explore engineering colleges in Maharashtra through **MHT-CET and Direct Second Year Engineering (DSE)** admission cutoff data.

## ✨ Features

* **MHT-CET & DSE Modes:** Explore first-year and direct second-year admission options.
* **College Predictor:** Compare student scores with available cutoff data.
* **Category Filters:** Find colleges based on reservation categories.
* **Branch Selection:** Filter engineering branches according to your interests.
* **Location Filters:** Search colleges by district or location.
* **Admission Chance Indicators:** View Safe, Target, and Reach categories.
* **College Comparison:** Compare potential college options.
* **CAP Option List:** Save and organize preferred colleges.
* **Export Options:** Export your preferred college list.
* **Dark & Light Themes:** Choose your preferred appearance.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* HTML5
* CSS3
* JavaScript
* Font Awesome
* Google Fonts

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open the project folder.
3. Open `index.html` in your web browser.
4. Select MHT-CET or DSE mode.
5. Enter your score and category.
6. Apply filters and explore matching colleges.

## 🎯 Project Goal

The goal is to make engineering college selection easier for students by organizing cutoff information and helping them prepare their CAP preference lists.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

Admission predictions are estimates based on available cutoff data. Actual allotments depend on the official admission rules, seat availability, category eligibility, and the current year's cutoffs. Always verify information with the official Maharashtra State CET Cell before finalizing admission choices.
